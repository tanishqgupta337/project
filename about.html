ABOUT US

Guiding you to the right answer

Beacon Help Center was built as a self-serve support hub — the kind of place you go when you have a quick question and don't want to wait on hold. It brings together frequently asked questions, category browsing, and an assistant that can point you to the right article in seconds.

Why "Beacon"?

Like a lighthouse guiding ships safely to shore, this help center is designed to guide you through account, billing, order, and security questions without friction — clearly labeled, easy to search, and available any time of day.

Built for learning, built to last

This project runs entirely in the browser: FAQs, categories, bookmarks and admin edits are all stored locally on your device, with no server or database required. It was designed as a demonstration of a modern, accessible, production-style front end.
