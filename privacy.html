Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This help center is a front-end demonstration project — everything described below applies to how the demo behaves in your browser.

What we store

All FAQ interactions — bookmarks, favorites, likes, search history, and theme or language preferences — are saved using your browser's LocalStorage. Nothing is transmitted to a server or third party.

Contact form

The contact and newsletter forms on this demo validate input locally and simulate a submission; no message data leaves your device.

Clearing your data

You can remove all stored preferences at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this page.

Changes to this policy

Since this is a static demo, this page will only change alongside updates to the project itself.
